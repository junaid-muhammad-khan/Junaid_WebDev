Nihari Recipe

For more free recipes, visit AllRecipes.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef or lamb
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 4 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 2 tablespoons Nihari masala
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 teaspoon garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Preparation Steps

  1. Heat oil or ghee in a pot and fry the onions until golden brown.
  2. Add ginger and garlic paste and cook for one minute.
  3. Add the meat and cook until it changes color.
  4. Add Nihari masala and salt, then mix well.
  5. Add water, cover, and cook on low heat for several hours.
  6. Cook until the meat is tender and the gravy thickens.
  7. Serve hot with naan or roti.
