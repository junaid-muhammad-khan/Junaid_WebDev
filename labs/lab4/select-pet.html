Choose a category that best describes your lifestyle:

Best for Apartments (Low Energy)

  • French Bulldog: Quiet, sturdy, and loves naps.
  • Pug: Affectionate and requires minimal exercise.
  • Cavalier King Charles: The ultimate lap dog.

Best for Active Families (High Energy)

  • Labrador Retriever: Friendly, athletic, and great with kids.
  • Border Collie: Highly intelligent; needs a job to do.
  • Golden Retriever: Patient, loyal, and loves outdoor play.

Best for Allergy Sufferers (Hypoallergenic)

  • Poodle: Comes in three sizes; very smart.
  • Bichon Frise: Playful, resilient, and doesn't shed.
  • Schnauzer: Protective and spirited with a wiry coat.
